# CONFIDENTIAL: Executive Compensation & Senior Leadership Incentive Plan

## RESTRICTED ACCESS: EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP AND BOARD OF DIRECTORS ONLY
Unauthorized dissemination, copying, or retrieval of this document constitutes a material violation of executive employment covenants and non-disclosure obligations.

## 1. Executive Salary Bands (Fiscal Year 2026)
- Chief Executive Officer (CEO): Base salary of $650,000 USD with a target performance equity grant of 250,000 RSUs vesting over 4 years.
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO): Base salary of $480,000 USD with an annual performance bonus multiplier up to 60% of base.
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO): Base salary of $450,000 USD with a retention incentive package of $150,000 payable at 24 months of tenure.

## 2. Change of Control Provisions (Golden Parachute)
- In the event of an acquisition or hostile change of control resulting in involuntary termination without cause, executive officers receive:
  - 24 months of full base salary continuation.
  - Immediate accelerated vesting of 100% of unvested stock options and RSUs.
  - 18 months of executive health benefit reimbursement.

## 3. Executive Discretionary Bonus Pool
- The Board Compensation Committee allocates an annual discretionary bonus pool capped at 12% of adjusted EBITDA.
- Individual allocations are calculated using the 3-point OKR matrix and approved by the Lead Independent Director in Q4 of each fiscal year.
